## What you may do

- **Self-host it as it is.** A company running BrainBox ATS for its own hiring
  may leave the product name, icons and wordmark in place, changed or not: the
  app is still BrainBox ATS, and its candidate-facing pages already show your
  company's own name and logo (see `COMPANY_*` in
  [docs/self-hosting.md](docs/self-hosting.md)).
- **Keep the "Powered by BrainBox" link** in the careers page footer.
- **Make accurate statements,** such as "based on BrainBox ATS", "a fork of
  BrainBox ATS" or "we use BrainBox ATS for hiring".
- **Refer to the project by name** when you write about it, review it or link
  to it.

## What you may not do

- **Use the BrainBox name or logo as the name or logo of your own product,**
  fork or modified distribution. If you distribute a modified version, rename
  it and replace the logos, icons and wordmarks listed above.
- Use the names or logos in a product name, company name, domain name or social
  media handle.
- Suggest that BrainBox Automations made, endorses, supports or is responsible
  for your version or your service when it isn't.

These rules sit alongside the license and apply regardless of what you build
with the code, including a competing product.
