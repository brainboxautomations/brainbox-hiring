## The two sentences that matter

From the license's "Noncompete" section:

> Any purpose is a permitted purpose, except for providing any product that competes with the software or any product the licensor or any of its affiliates provides using the software.

From its "Competition" section:

> Goods and services compete even when provided free of charge.

> If you market a product as a practical substitute for the software or another product, it definitely competes.

The licensor is BrainBox Automations. In short: use it, change it and run it
for your own hiring as much as you like. Don't turn it into an ATS product or
service for other organisations.

## At a glance

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| A company self-hosting it for its own hiring, free of charge | Selling or offering it as an ATS product or service for other organisations, paid or free |
| Commercial use: a business using it to hire its own staff | Hosting it for other companies, or running a hosted service that serves more than one company |
| Modifying it for your own hiring | Rebranding it and offering it to others as your own ATS |
| A recruitment agency using it as its internal tool to hire for client companies, **with no logins or access for the clients** | A recruitment agency giving its client companies their own logins or access |

| Sharing copies, changed or not, with the license and its `Required Notice:` line, for uses the license permits | Offering it, or something built from it, as a substitute for BrainBox Automations' own offering |

## Questions and answers

### Can my company use BrainBox ATS for its own hiring?

Yes. Any company may self-host it and use it for its own hiring, free of
charge, including a commercial business hiring for itself. There is no fee, no
user limit and nothing to sign.

### Can we change the code?

Yes. You may modify it for your own hiring: new fields, a different look, an
integration with your HR system. Your changes are yours to keep private.

### Can we share our modified version?

Yes, for purposes the license permits. Anyone who gets a copy from you must
also get the license terms (or their URL) and the `Required Notice:` line at
the top of [LICENSE](LICENSE), and they are bound by the same terms. Don't
distribute it under the BrainBox name or logo; see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

### We are a recruitment agency. Can we use it for our clients' hiring?

Yes, as your **internal tool**. Your recruiters may use it to run hiring on
behalf of your client companies, as long as **the client companies do not get
their own logins or any other access to it**.

Giving clients logins means you are providing an ATS to other companies, and
that is not allowed.

### I'm a freelancer or consultant. Can I be paid to set it up for a company?

Yes. You may be paid to install, set up and customise BrainBox ATS on a
company's **own infrastructure** (its own Supabase project, its own hosting
accounts). That company is then an ordinary self-hoster using it for its own
hiring.

What you may not do is turn it into your product: offering "your" ATS, under any
name, that you install, host or resell for client after client, or hosting it
for clients on your own infrastructure. That is providing an ATS to other
organisations.

### Can I offer it as a hosted service?

No. Running BrainBox ATS, or something built from it, as a hosted service for
other organisations is providing a competing product, whether you charge for it
or not, and whether it serves one company or many.

### Can I rebrand it and sell it, or give it away, as an ATS?

No. Selling or offering it as an ATS product for other organisations, hosted,
installed or rebranded, paid or free, is not allowed. Offering it as a
substitute for BrainBox Automations' own offering is not allowed either.

### Can I use parts of the code in another product?

Only in a product that does not compete with BrainBox ATS or with a product
BrainBox Automations provides using it. Using parts of it in another ATS or
recruiting product is not allowed.

### What if BrainBox Automations later launches something that competes with what I'm doing?

The license's "New Products" section covers this: you may keep using the
versions released before that happened, but not later versions.

### What happens if I break the license by mistake?

The license's "Violations" section gives you 32 days after you are first
notified in writing to come into full compliance and take practical steps to
correct past violations. If you do, your license continues.

### Is BrainBox ATS open source?

No. The source code is public and free to use for the purposes above, but the
license restricts competing uses, so it is source-available rather than open
source in the sense of the Open Source Definition.

### Do I need to sign anything to use it?

No. The [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) applies only when you
contribute code to this repository.

### Can I use the BrainBox name or logo?

The license covers the code, not the name or logo. See
[TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

### Is there a hosted version?

BrainBox Automations plans to offer one. See
[brainboxautomations.com](https://www.brainboxautomations.com).
